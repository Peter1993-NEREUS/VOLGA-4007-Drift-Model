DECREE OF THE PRESIDENT OF THE RUSSIAN FEDERATION

On Approval of the List of Information in the Fuel and Energy Sector Access to Which Is Restricted, and the Procedure for Its Use

Official Russian publication
Reference translation only. In case of any discrepancy, the Russian-language original prevails.

In view of the need to take urgent measures in response to unfriendly actions of the United States of America and foreign states and international organizations that have joined them, which are contrary to international law, and in accordance with Parts 1 and 2 of Article 9 of Federal Law No. 149-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Information, Information Technologies and Protection of Information” and Article 4² of Federal Law No. 127-FZ of 4 June 2018 “On Measures of Influence (Counteraction) in Response to Unfriendly Actions of the United States of America and Other Foreign States”, I hereby decree:

1. To approve the attached list of information in the fuel and energy sector access to which is restricted.

2. To establish that compliance with the confidentiality of the information included in the list approved by this Decree and implementation of the measures necessary for this purpose, as provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation, shall be mandatory.

3. To establish the following procedure for the use of information included in the list approved by this Decree:

a) dissemination of information included in the list approved by this Decree, including through mass media or information and telecommunications networks, including the Internet, shall not be permitted, except for information about its own activities disclosed by a legal entity;

b) provision of information included in the list approved by this Decree shall be carried out on the grounds and in accordance with the procedure provided for by an agreement concluded between the holder of such information and the person (persons) receiving access to it, except for the cases specified in subparagraph “c” of this paragraph;

c) provision of information included in the list approved by this Decree by federal executive authorities, other federal state bodies and state authorities of the constituent entities of the Russian Federation within their competence, as well as provision of such information to the said bodies, shall be carried out on the grounds and in accordance with the procedure provided for by the legislation of the Russian Federation;

d) access to information included in the list approved by this Decree and contained in state information systems of federal executive authorities shall be provided in accordance with the procedure established by the legislation of the Russian Federation;

e) provision of information included in the list approved by this Decree and obtained, taking into account the information-security requirements established by the legislation of the Russian Federation, from state information systems of federal executive authorities shall be carried out in accordance with the standard regulations for automated processing of information obtained from state information systems of federal executive authorities, to be approved by the Government of the Russian Federation, unless otherwise provided by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

4. The Government of the Russian Federation shall:

a) within 10 days determine the list of commodity codes in accordance with the Unified Commodity Nomenclature of Foreign Economic Activity of the Eurasian Economic Union and the All-Russian Classification of Products by Economic Activity in respect of which the provisions of this Decree apply;

b) approve standard regulations for automated processing of information obtained from state information systems of federal executive authorities;

c) bring its acts into conformity with this Decree.

5. This Decree shall enter into force on the date of its signing.

President of the Russian Federation V. Putin
Moscow, Kremlin
28 September 2026
No. 686

LIST
of Information in the Fuel and Energy Sector Access to Which Is Restricted

1. Information on the export of goods by a legal entity, including:

a) names and quantities of goods being exported;

b) information on the price of goods, including the average price determined by the manufacturer for industrial goods (services) intended for export;

c) information on sellers and buyers of goods, as well as other persons participating in or facilitating the export of goods, including carriers, freight forwarders and insurers;

d) the procedure for settlements under contracts for the supply of goods;

e) information on vehicles used for the carriage of goods by sea, container numbers and details of transport documents;

f) information on supply destinations, transport routes, places of transfer, shipment, transshipment, destination and export of goods, including the names of marine terminals at which shipment, transshipment and/or technological accumulation of goods is carried out, the names of the operators of such marine terminals, as well as information on the geographical coordinates of warehouses and storage areas used for accumulation of goods being exported;

g) information on the date and time of commencement and completion of operations involving goods being exported;

h) information contained in documents required for customs operations in connection with the export of goods from the Russian Federation.

2. Aggregated customs statistics concerning the export of goods by a legal entity.

3. Information on the volumes of processing and production of goods by a legal entity that is a Russian oil refinery.

4. Information on planned volumes of sale of goods and on the volumes of transactions concluded at exchange trading, during the main trading sessions of each trading day of the relevant tax period, by a legal entity and/or a legal entity belonging to the same group of persons with it in accordance with the antimonopoly legislation of the Russian Federation, for the sale of certain types of petroleum products (high-octane motor gasoline with a Research Octane Number of 92 or higher, and diesel fuel, the release into circulation and circulation of which are permitted in the territory of the Russian Federation), produced by the legal entity from petroleum feedstock extracted and/or sold by an economic entity occupying a dominant position in the relevant commodity market, including persons belonging to the same group of persons with such economic entity and/or exchange-trading participants acting in the interests and at the expense of such economic entity or the said persons.
